What it is and who it is for

Publicador Videos is a personal tool that uploads the owner's videos to the accounts @unporquecadadia, @radarmeta / @radar.meta, @gym_conciencia / @gym.conciencia. Only their owner uses it, for those accounts. It is not an open service: there is no sign-up, no user accounts and no payments.

Permitted use

  • Only original content is published, to which the owner holds all rights (video, music, voice, images and text).
  • Every video is reviewed before it is published. On TikTok it is sent to drafts and the final post is made by hand in the app.
  • Each network's rules are followed: the TikTok Community Guidelines, the Instagram Community Guidelines, the YouTube Community Guidelines and each platform's developer terms.
  • It is not used for spam, mass posting, impersonation or managing other people's accounts.

Platform terms

Using the tool means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and the Instagram Terms of Use. Where any of them conflicts with these terms, the platform's terms prevail.

Data

How data is handled is explained in the privacy policy.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is. It relies on third-party APIs that may change or become unavailable; if a network rejects a post, the tool stops and does not retry on its own.
