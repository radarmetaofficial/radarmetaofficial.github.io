Who uses it and who is responsible

Publicador Videos is a personal tool. It is used by one person only, the owner of the accounts @unporquecadadia, @radarmeta / @radar.meta, @gym_conciencia / @gym.conciencia (the “operator”), to publish their own videos on their own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else and has no outside users.

Operator contact: radarmeta.official@gmail.com.

What data it handles

NetworkDataPurpose
Instagram (Meta)Instagram user ID, username and the account's access token.Knowing which account to post to and publishing its Reels with their caption.
TikTokopen_id, display name and avatar (user.info.basic scope); access and refresh tokens.Identifying the account and sending its videos to drafts (video.upload scope).
YouTube (Google)Channel ID and title; OAuth access and refresh tokens.Uploading videos to the brand's channel.

Each network also receives the video and its caption, which are the operator's own content. The tool does not read followers, messages, comments or analytics, and handles no data about anyone else.

Where it is kept and for how long

  • Everything is stored only on the operator's computer, in a folder of their user profile outside any project or repository. There is no server, online database or cloud copy.
  • Instagram: the token lasts 60 days and is renewed automatically every week while in use.
  • TikTok: the access token lasts 24 hours and the refresh token about a year.
  • YouTube: the token is kept until permission is revoked.
  • A local log records the date of each renewal and whether it succeeded; it contains no tokens.
  • When permission is revoked or the tool is no longer used, the files are deleted.

Who it is shared with

No one but the network itself: each piece of data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (Meta, TikTok or Google), and only to publish on the matching account. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or transferred to other services.

How to revoke permission

  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Active → Publicador Videos-IG → Remove.
  • TikTok: Profile → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services → Publicador Videos → Remove access.
  • YouTube / Google: on your Google Account permissions page, myaccount.google.com/connections (or security.google.com/settings/security/permissions), choose Publicador Videos and remove access.

You can also ask for the data to be deleted by writing to the contact above; it will be done within 30 days.

Platform rules

Publicador Videos uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and Instagram's Terms of Use and Meta's Privacy Policy also apply.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics, tracking, or third-party fonts, videos or scripts: everything is served from here. The host (GitHub Pages) may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

If you believe the tool handles any data about you, you can request access, correction or deletion by writing to radarmeta.official@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

The tool is not aimed at children and collects no data about children.

Changes

If what the tool handles changes (for example, if it one day posts directly on TikTok), this page will be updated and the date above changed before the change takes effect.
